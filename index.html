09 / Questions

Before we begin.

What does the ₹4,999 starting price include?

It is the starting price for a professional website of up to five pages with responsive design, contact and WhatsApp links, an enquiry form, basic on-page SEO setup, testing and deployment. Final scope is confirmed in writing before work starts.

Are domain and hosting included?

Not automatically. Domain registration, hosting, paid tools and third-party services are discussed separately so you know the cost and who owns each account.

Can you build an online store or booking system?

These features can be discussed as custom requirements. Their cost and timeline depend on the workflow, integrations, payment provider and administration needs.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the number of pages, content readiness, design complexity, integrations and feedback. A project timeline is shared after the requirements are understood.

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of the website approach, with testing across common screen sizes before launch.
